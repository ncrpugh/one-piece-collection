# Project Vision

## Purpose
A One Piece TCG collection app that allows you to easily keep track of your physical collection, enabling quick and seamless deck construction and management of your cards.

The app aims to provide a simple and intuitive way to look up and add cards, while presenting useful information about your collection. This could include the percentage of a set you own, how close you are to completing a master set, and whether you have enough cards to build a particular deck.

The goal is to make managing a physical collection quick and straightforward, while also making the information stored about that collection useful and easy to understand.
